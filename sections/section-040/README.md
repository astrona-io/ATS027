# Utilize persistent and ephemeral volumes

This section covers utilize persistent and ephemeral volumes. You will study Volume Lifetime And Provisioning, Access Modes And Data Retention. The lessons explain the responsible components, configuration relationships and failure cases relevant to application development.

Each module begins with its goals and ends with a summary. Reusable YAML knowledge checks follow the section summary and are registered separately for future presentation.

## Learning Outcomes

- Distinguish writable layers, emptyDir and persistent claims.
- Explain volume survival across restart and replacement.
- Trace claim binding and dynamic provisioning.
- Diagnose unavailable storage dependencies.
- Interpret volume access and presentation modes.
- Distinguish node access from application concurrency.
- Predict claim and volume deletion consequences.
- Separate persistent availability from backup and recovery.

Each module pairs its concept readings with a guided practical sequence in an isolated playground. Finish the practice and closing summary before moving on. The YAML knowledge checks remain separate from the environment lifecycle.
