# Choose and use the right workload resource (Deployment, DaemonSet, CronJob, etc.)

This section covers choose and use the right workload resource (Deployment, DaemonSet, CronJob, etc.). You will study Controllers For Continuous Applications, Finite And Scheduled Application Work. The lessons explain the responsible components, configuration relationships and failure cases relevant to application development.

Each module begins with its goals and ends with a summary. Reusable YAML knowledge checks follow the section summary and are registered separately for future presentation.

## Learning Outcomes

- Compare Pod ownership and reconciliation.
- Choose a Deployment for interchangeable replicas.
- Choose a DaemonSet for node-local responsibility.
- Explain StatefulSet identity and storage relationships.
- Distinguish a Job from a long-running service.
- Explain completions, parallelism and retries.
- Compare CronJob overlap policies.
- Evaluate deadlines, idempotence and cleanup.

Each module pairs its concept readings with a guided practical sequence in an isolated playground. Finish the practice and closing summary before moving on. The YAML knowledge checks remain separate from the environment lifecycle.
