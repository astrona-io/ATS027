# Prerequisites

- The `astrona` command-line tool, installed and working.
- A container engine that kind can use (Podman or Docker) with about 2 GiB of free memory.
- `kubectl`.
- You know what Deployments, DaemonSets, StatefulSets, Jobs and CronJobs guarantee, and how a Deployment rolling update, a headless Service and a volume claim template work.

The lab starts a one-node kind cluster running Kubernetes 1.35.
